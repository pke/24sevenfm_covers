Audio

Audio is optional — leave it off to use the page as a silent cover display next to your own player. Streams are AAC; if your browser can't play them, use the playlist files in a media player instead.

Visualizations

On 1980s.FM, a WebGL neon dance floor, a 32-band spectrum wall, laser snaps and diffraction bursts follow stable kicks and their estimated BPM. Beat-light songs ease into a quieter floor with slowly circling laser patterns. Browsers without Web Audio use that calm mode too. The optional strobe adds occasional double flashes on strong phrase beats; leave it off if you are sensitive to flashing lights. Motion and flashes are disabled when your system requests reduced motion. Optional CO₂ particle bursts fire inward from the front corners, then billow upward instead of adding a permanent fog layer.

Rating badges use the selected cover transition, stay visible for ten seconds after a track change, and then behave like the stage controls. Enabling this sends the current and upcoming queued titles to the resolver.
