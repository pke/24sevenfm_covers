Ratings

Show

Show German FSK and/or US ratings for the matched movie or series. Rating badges use the selected cover transition, stay visible for ten seconds after a track change, and then behave like the stage controls. Enabling this sends the current and upcoming queued titles to the resolver.

80s laser show

A WebGL neon dance floor, a 32-band spectrum wall, laser snaps and diffraction bursts follow stable kicks and their estimated BPM. Beat-light songs ease into a quieter floor with slowly circling laser patterns. Browsers without Web Audio use that calm mode too. The optional strobe adds occasional double flashes on strong phrase beats; leave it off if you are sensitive to flashing lights. Motion and flashes are disabled when your system requests reduced motion. Optional CO₂ particle bursts fire inward from the front corners, then billow upward.
